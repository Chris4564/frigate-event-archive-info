Frigate Event Archive

A personal utility that copies completed Frigate camera event clips to the account owner's Google Drive. It runs on the owner's server and uses rclone to transfer files.

The archive requests permission to create and manage only the Google Drive files it creates. Recordings are private unless the account owner chooses to share them. This website does not show or receive recordings.

Privacy policy · Support and questions
