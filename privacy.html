Frigate Event Archive

Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026.

Purpose and information used

This personal utility copies completed camera event clips, their filenames, event times and object labels from the account owner's Frigate server to that owner's Google Drive. It uses Google OAuth authorisation and the drive.file permission to create and manage files created by the utility. It does not request access to unrelated Drive files, Gmail or contacts.

Storage and sharing

Google authorisation tokens and the archive ledger are stored privately on the owner's server. Clips are transferred to Google Drive using encrypted connections. The utility does not sell data, send it to advertising services, or share it with other users. Google processes uploaded files under its own terms and privacy policy. This public information website does not receive clips or authorisation tokens and uses no analytics, tracking scripts or cookies of its own. Its hosting provider may process ordinary website access logs.

Retention and deletion

The configured cloud retention is 30 days. The utility moves its expired clips to Google Drive's trash; Google Drive's normal trash retention applies. It does not empty trash or remove unrelated files. Local Frigate event recording retention is two days. Local export copies are removed after the cloud copy's size and checksum have been verified; unuploaded exports remain queued subject to available storage.

Your control

The account owner can delete archived clips directly in Google Drive, stop the archive service, or revoke its authorisation in Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future transfers but does not automatically remove existing clips. Tokens and the ledger can also be removed from the owner's server.

Google API policy

Frigate Event Archive's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The utility does not use Google user data to train artificial intelligence models.

Contact

For questions or deletion help, use the project support page. Do not include credentials, camera footage or other private information in a public issue.
